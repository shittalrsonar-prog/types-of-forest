About Our Project

Our interdisciplinary project explores different types of forests through science, mathematics, languages, social science and creative learning.

Forests are important ecosystems that support biodiversity, maintain environmental balance and provide habitats for countless living organisms.

Explore Forest Types

Discover some major forest ecosystems around the world.

Tropical Forest

Forests that shed their leaves during the dry season.

Temperate Forest

Forests found in regions with seasonal changes in temperature.

Boreal Forest(Taiga)

Forests dominated by cone-bearing trees such as pine and fir.

Mangrove Forest

Coastal forests adapted to salty water and tidal conditions.

Dry Forest

Dry-region forests with plants adapted to limited rainfall.

Our Subjects

Discover how different school subjects help us understand forests, nature, and the environment.

Science

Explore forest ecosystems, biodiversity, plants, animals, and the importance of conservation.

Mathematics

Understand forest data through graphs, statistics, measurements, and calculations.

English

Express the beauty of forests through creative writing, poems, and environmental messages.

Hindi

Learn about forests through Hindi language, literature, and nature-related expressions.

Gujarati

Explore forests and environmental awareness through Gujarati language and literature.

Sanskrit

Discover the connection between nature, trees, and environmental values through Sanskrit.

Social Science

Understand forest resources, human dependence, conservation, and environmental responsibility.
